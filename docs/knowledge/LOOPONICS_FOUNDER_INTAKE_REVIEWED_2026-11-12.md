## D. Delivery process

### Q15. What actually happens during discovery?
**Status: FOUNDER-CONFIRMED**

**Answer:** Looponics begins by discussing the client's idea and intended business outcome. The team seeks to understand business logic, functional and nonfunctional requirements, users, existing workflows, technical constraints, and expected results. Where useful and authorized, the team may seek input from relevant business stakeholders and examine the working context. Structured questionnaires/forms are used to reveal missing requirements and identify follow-up questions. The gathered information is analyzed against feasibility, available budget, expected timeline, and project priorities before a proposed scope is finalized.

### Q16. What planning/strategy outputs do clients receive?
**Status: FOUNDER-CONFIRMED for intended proposal workflow; exact templates not yet supplied**

**Answer:** Following discovery, Looponics prepares an appropriately detailed **technical/project proposal, budget estimate, scope and delivery plan**, with architecture considerations and a proposed timeline where relevant. The client and agency then work toward a written **agreement** documenting expectations, deliverables, scope boundaries, commercial terms, and responsibilities in understandable language. Additional client requests are assessed for feasibility and applicable company/platform policies; infeasible requests are discussed with the client rather than silently accepted.

**Contract caution:** A proposed budget/timeline is not a binding commitment until properly approved by the contracting parties.

### Q17. How do design/build reviews and client approvals work?
**Status: PARTIALLY DEFINED**

**Answer:** Each engagement is intended to have a project workspace in a project management tool where the team records work status and shares progress with the client. **Weekly check-in meetings** are planned to review deliverables, discuss open questions, collect feedback, and address client priorities. Clients may also send messages or questions between scheduled meetings through agreed channels.

**Still to confirm:** The selected tool, named approvers, approval evidence (email/ticket/signature), revision limits, escalation procedure, and how delayed client feedback affects schedule. “Client can message at any time” does **not** equal guaranteed 24/7 support or immediate replies.

### Q18. Who deploys and owns production infrastructure?
**Status: PARTIALLY DEFINED**

**Answer:** The founder intends that the **client owns the delivered website**. Looponics' engineering team will perform or coordinate deployment and hosting configuration using infrastructure accounts/access made available by the client. The client will provide required account access and deployment information.

**Important clarification:** Website/product ownership is not automatically identical to ownership of the hosting account, domains, source-code copyright, pre-existing agency components, third-party libraries, or licensed third-party services. These rights and responsibilities must be documented in the agreement before work begins.

**RECOMMENDATION (NOT APPROVED):** Prefer client-controlled accounts and time-limited, least-privilege delegated access over handing over primary account passwords. Specify who pays renewal bills, manages backups, monitors environments, and can revoke access.
