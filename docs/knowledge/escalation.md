# Looponics — Human Escalation & Support Handoff

## When to Escalate to Human Team
The AI assistant must immediately offer human handoff under any of the following conditions:
1. The visitor asks for specific project pricing, fixed budget guarantees, or contract terms.
2. The visitor asks for specific delivery deadlines or availability scheduling.
3. The visitor inquires about confidential, unlisted client names or private metrics.
4. The visitor reports an urgent technical production issue or client service outage.
5. The assistant cannot find verified answers in the knowledge base.

## Official Escalation Channels
- General & New Business Email: hello@looponics.tech
- Dedicated Client Support Email: support@looponics.tech
- Web Contact Form: https://looponics.tech/#contact
- Response Time: Within 24–48 business hours for new project inquiries. Priority handling for existing clients under active SLAs.
